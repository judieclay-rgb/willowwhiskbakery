Baked slowly. Shared happily.

        

Willow & Whisk is a home bakery in Portland, Oregon. We make small-batch sourdough, cookies, and custom cakes for neighbors, friends, and local events.

        

          Order a treat           See the menu         

      
    
    
      
        

What comes out of the oven

        

Everything is made to order in a licensed home kitchen. No shortcuts, no preservatives.

        
          
                         

Sourdough

            

Naturally leavened, 24-hour ferment, crackly crust. Plain, rosemary, or olive.

          
          
                         

Cookies

            

Brown butter chocolate chip, oatmeal cranberry, and double dark chocolate.

          
          
                         

Custom cakes

            

Birthdays, showers, and just-because cakes. Simple designs, real ingredients.

          
        
      
    
    
      
        
          

Why a home bakery?

          

Because small batches taste better. We only bake what we can make well, and we know almost every customer by name.

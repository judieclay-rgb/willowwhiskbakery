About the bakery

        

One oven, two hands, and a lot of butter.

      
    
    
      
                 
          

Hi, I'm Maya.

          

I started Willow & Whisk in 2021 after years of baking for friends and coworkers. What began as a weekend hobby turned into a licensed home kitchen, a weekly bake list, and a community of people who really love bread.

          

I grew up in a family where nothing was measured exactly and everything was tasted along the way. That's still how I bake. I use local flour, real butter, and a sourdough starter I've kept alive since the beginning.

          

When I'm not baking, I'm probably reading, hiking with my dog, or testing a new cookie recipe on my neighbors.

        
      
    
    
      
        

What we care about

        
          
            

Small batches

            

We bake to order so nothing sits around. It means limited quantities and fresher everything.

          
          
            

Local ingredients

            

Flour from Camas Country Mill, butter from a Oregon creamery, and produce from the farmers market.

          
          
            

Honest pricing

            

No hidden fees, no inflated "artisan" markups. Just fair prices for food made with care.

          
        
      
    
    
      
        

Want to try something?

        

Start with a loaf of sourdough or a dozen cookies. We think you'll come back.
